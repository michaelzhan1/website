A logic-based sudoku solver that uses techniques rather than brute-force DFS.
I've had a sudoku app on my phone that I've played countless times for years out
of boredom, and I wanted to finally get something out of it. In writing this
project, I really started to appreciate how nice Go feels to write in terms of
its semantics and project organization, to the point where it's probably my
favorite language now. I also wrote a blog post about this project
[here](/blog/2026-10-10-sudoku).
