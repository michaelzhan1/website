One of my friends in my Masters program gave a lecture series about mathematical
visualization, and one of the topics was about maze generation. This inspired me
to code up my own little maze generation, which uses randomized DFS. There are
other approaches such as the MST algorithms (Kruskal's and Prim's) and other
algorithms I've never heard of before, but I haven't dug that deep into them
yet.
